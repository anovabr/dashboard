        Why this board works — the psychology
        • This Week stays short. A capped focus list fights choice overload — five things you can actually finish beats seventeen you can't.
        • ☆ pinning = implementation intentions. Deciding when you'll do a thing (this week, not "someday") is one of the best-studied ways to actually do it.
        • Red vs. purple dates. External deadlines and your own aims are different psychological objects — the board never lets them masquerade as each other.
        • The ring and the Done archive make progress visible. Small wins are the single strongest daily motivator — so finished work stays on the board where you can see it.
        • Monday review = fresh start. Weekly resets exploit the fresh-start effect: new week, clean slate, recommitment.
